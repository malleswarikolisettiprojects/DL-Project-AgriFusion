# Blackgram (Urdbean / Minumulu) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Vigna mungo* (L.) Hepper (Blackgram / Urdbean / Black Lentil)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** మినుములు (Minumulu), మినపప్పు (Minapappu), ఉలవలు/మినుము (Urdbean)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Seed Treatment

### Soil & Season Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Well-drained fertile loamy to heavy black cotton soils with optimal pH between 6.5 and 7.8. Sensitive to waterlogging and salinity.
- **Sowing Seasons:**
  - **Kharif:** June – July (Rainfed & Irrigated)
  - **Rabi:** October – November (Residual moisture / Irrigated / Rice fallows)
  - **Summer:** January – February (Post-harvest rice fallow in Krishna-Godavari & AP coastal delta)

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Rainfed / Kharif:** 15 – 20 kg/ha. Spacing: 30 cm (row-to-row) x 10 cm (plant-to-plant).
- **Irrigated / Rabi Rice Fallows:** 20 – 25 kg/ha (Broadcasting in standing rice crop 3–5 days before harvest or line sowing). Spacing: 30 cm x 10 cm.

### Seed Treatment Protocol
- **Fungal Protection:** Treat seeds with *Carbendazim 50% WP* @ 2 g/kg seed or *Thiram 75% WS* @ 2 g/kg seed.  
  *Organic Bio-control:* Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* (1% WP, Accession ITCC-6914) @ 4 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.
- **Sucking Pest Protection:** Seed treatment with *Imidacloprid 70% WS* @ 5 g/kg seed to protect seedlings against whiteflies, aphids, and stem fly for the first 25–30 days.
- **Rhizobium Inoculation:** Inoculate treated seeds with *Rhizobium leguminosarum* pulse strain culture @ 200 g / 10 kg seed using rice kanji as adhesive.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Application

### Fertilizer Application Rates (NPKS)

#### A. Rainfed Crop (NPKS Rate)
- **NPKS Requirement:** 12.5 kg N : 25 kg P2O5 : 12.5 kg K2O : 10 kg S per hectare.
- **Application:** Apply full dose of 12.5 kg N, 25 kg P2O5, 12.5 kg K2O, and 10 kg Gypsum/Sulfur as basal at the time of sowing.

#### B. Irrigated / Rabi Crop (NPKS Rate)
- **NPKS Requirement:** 25 kg N : 50 kg P2O5 : 25 kg K2O : 20 kg S per hectare.
- **Basal Application:** Apply full P, K, S, and 50% N (12.5 kg) at sowing.
- **Top Dressing:** Apply remaining 12.5 kg N at 25–30 days after sowing (DAS).

### Micronutrient & Foliar Nutrition
- **Zinc Deficiency (Chlorosis in young leaves):** Basal soil application of Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) @ 25 kg/ha for irrigated blackgram. Foliar spray of 0.5% ZnSO4 (5 g/L) at 25–30 DAS.
- **Foliar N Boost:** Foliar spray of 1% Urea solution (10 g/L) or 2% DAP solution (20 g/L) at peak flowering stage (30 DAS) and pod formation stage (45 DAS) to increase pod set and seed weight.
- **Boron & Molybdenum Correction:** *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar application rates for Boron/Molybdenum in Telangana/AP blackgram soils are missing from reviewed KVK bulletins; consult local KVK or PJTSAU/ANGRAU extension officer before applying.]*

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **FYM / Compost:** Apply Farm Yard Manure (FYM) @ 12.5 t/ha or Vermicompost @ 2.5 t/ha during final land preparation.
- **Bio-Fertilizer Soil Application:** Apply *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/ha + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/ha mixed with 50 kg FYM basal.

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Protection

### Cultural & Mechanical Measures
- Erect **Yellow Sticky Traps** @ 15/acre for monitoring and trapping whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*) and aphids.
- Erect **Blue Sticky Traps** @ 10/acre for thrips (*Thrips palmi*).
- Erect **Pheromone Traps** @ 5/acre for *Maruca vitrata* (Spotted Pod Borer) and *Helicoverpa armigera*.
- Erect bird perches @ 10–15/acre for natural predatory birds.

### Major Pests & Control Protocols

#### A. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*) & Aphids (*Aphis craccivora*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Sap sucking, leaves curling downward, honey-dew secretion attracting sooty mold, vectoring Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV).
- **Organic Option:** Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) @ 50 g/L or Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L) or Azadirachtin 1% EC @ 2.5 mL/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.2 g/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Mandatory 7–10 days wait before pod harvest. *(Label & CIBRC confirmation required)*.

#### B. Thrips (*Thrips palmi* / *Caliothrips indicus*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Leaves show silvering, crinkling, cupping upward, malformed flowers.
- **Organic Option:** Spray NSKE 5% or *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 mL/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L.

#### C. Spotted Pod Borer (*Maruca vitrata*) & Gram Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Webbing of leaves and inflorescence, boreholes on pods, larva feeding on developing seeds.
- **Biological Option:** Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 1 kg/ha or NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) @ 250 LE/ha at flowering stage.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 mL/L or Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 0.2 mL/L.
- **PHI:** Minimum 7 days.

#### D. Stem Fly (*Ophiomyia phaseoli*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Seedlings wilt, lower stem swells, red discoloration inside stem tissues.
- **Chemical Option:** Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 5 g/kg seed. Prophylactic foliar spray of Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L at 10–14 DAS.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Yellow Mosaic Virus (YMV)
- **Symptoms:** Yellow patches on young leaves merging to give complete yellowing, stunting, pod size reduction. Transmitted by Whiteflies (*Bemisia tabaci*).
- **Management:** Vector Control (Whitefly management with Neem oil / Imidacloprid). Rogue out and destroy YMV-infected plants immediately. Use resistant/tolerant varieties (e.g., LBG-752, PU-31, TBG-104, WBG-26). No chemical curative spray exists.

### B. Powdery Mildew (*Erysiphe polygoni*)
- **Symptoms:** White flour-like powdery patches on upper leaf surface, petioles, and pods during cool dry weather.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Wettable Sulfur 80% WP @ 2 g/L or Sulfur dust @ 25 kg/ha.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 mL/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L.

### C. Cercospora Leaf Spot (*Cercospora canescens*)
- **Symptoms:** Circular to irregular reddish-brown spots with grayish centers on leaves, causing premature leaf fall.
- **Foliar Spray:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/L or Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L at 10-day intervals.

### D. Root Rot & Wilt (*Rhizoctonia solani* / *Fusarium oxysporum*)
- **Symptoms:** Seedling blight, damping off, collar rot, reddish-brown root lesions, plant wilting.
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Trichoderma viride* @ 5 kg/ha mixed with 500 kg organic manure/FYM.
- **Chemical Option:** Soil drenching at root zone with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Timing:** Harvest when 80–90% of pods turn black and dry. Avoid late picking to prevent pod shattering.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI):**
  - Chemical Insecticides (Emamectin / Chlorantraniliprole): **7 days** minimum gap before harvest.
  - Chemical Fungicides (Mancozeb / Carbendazim): **7 – 10 days** minimum gap before harvest.
  - Bio-pesticides (Neem Oil, Bt, Trichoderma): **0 – 1 day** safety margin.
- **Storage Protection:** Dry seeds to 8–9% moisture level before storage. Mix seeds with Neem leaf powder @ 10 g/kg seed or apply pulse storage oil to prevent pulse beetle (*Callosobruchus maculatus*) damage.
