# Rice (Oryza sativa / వరి / బియ్యం) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Oryza sativa* L. (Paddy / Rice / వరి)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** వరి (Vari / Paddy), బియ్యం (Biyyam / Rice), వరి పైరు (Vari Pairu / Rice Crop)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Land Preparation

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Well-drained clay loams, silty clay loams, and alluvial soils with high water retention capacity. Optimal soil pH: 5.5 – 7.0. Tolerates slightly alkaline soils but sensitive to severe salinity (EC > 4 dS/m).
- **Sowing & Planting Seasons:**
  - **Kharif (Sarva / Main Season):** Sowing in June – July; transplanting in July – August.
  - **Rabi / Yasangi (Dalwa Season):** Sowing in November – December; transplanting in December – January.

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Transplanted Rice:** 20 – 25 kg/acre (50 kg/ha) for open-pollinated varieties; 6 – 8 kg/acre (15–20 kg/ha) for F1 Hybrids.
- **Direct Seeded Rice (DSR / Drum Seeder):** 12 – 15 kg/acre (30–35 kg/ha).
- **System of Rice Intensification (SRI):** 2.0 – 2.5 kg/acre (5–6 kg/ha).
- **Spacing:** 
  - Kharif: 20 cm x 15 cm (33 hills/m²).
  - Rabi / Yasangi: 15 cm x 15 cm (44 hills/m²) for higher hill density during cooler vegetative growth.

### Seed Treatment & Nursery Inoculation
- **Bio-Control Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4–5 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed in 1 L water to prevent seed-borne fungal infections.
- **Chemical Seed Treatment:** Soak seeds in *Carbendazim 50% WP* @ 2 g/kg seed or *Thiram 75% WS* @ 3 g/kg seed for 12 hours before nursery sowing.
- **Bacterial Leaf Blight Prevention:** Soak seeds in a solution of *Streptocycline* @ 0.1 g/L + *Copper Oxychloride 50% WP* @ 1 g/L for 12 hours.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Recommended NPK Fertilizer Rates
- **Irrigated High Yielding Varieties (HYV):** 120 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40–50 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Hybrid Rice Varieties:** 150 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 60 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Application Schedule:**
  - **Basal Application:** Apply full dose of P2O5 (60 kg/ha), 50% of K2O (20–30 kg/ha), and 25% of N (30 kg/ha) at the time of final puddling/land preparation.
  - **1st Top Dressing (Active Tillering - 20 to 25 DAT):** Apply 50% of N (60 kg/ha).
  - **2nd Top Dressing (Panicle Initiation - 40 to 45 DAT):** Apply remaining 25% of N (30 kg/ha) and remaining 50% of K2O (20–30 kg/ha).

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Apply FYM / Compost @ 10 tonnes/ha during preliminary land preparation.
- **Green Manuring:** Incorporate green manure crops like *Dhaincha* (*Sesbania aculeata*) or *Sunnhemp* (*Crotalaria juncea*) @ 25 kg seed/ha at 45 days (before puddling) to add ~60 kg N/ha naturally.
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Soil application of *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/acre + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/acre mixed in 100 kg FYM.
- **Blue-Green Algae (BGA) & Azolla:** Apply fresh *Azolla pinnata* @ 10 t/ha as dual cropping or BGA @ 10 kg/ha 7 days after transplanting in standing water.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Zinc Deficiency ("Khaira" Disease / Brown Leaf Bronzing):**
  - **Soil Application (Basal):** Apply Zinc Sulphate Heptahydrate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 25 kg/ha (10 kg/acre) or Zinc Sulphate Monohydrate (33%) @ 15 kg/ha at final puddling. Do not mix directly with phosphatic fertilizers.
  - **Foliar Correction:** Spray 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (5 g/L) + 1.0% Urea (10 g/L) twice at 15-day intervals starting from 15–20 DAT if deficiency symptoms appear.
- **Iron Deficiency (Chlorosis in Light Soils / DSR):** Foliar spray of 1.0% Ferrous Sulphate (10 g/L) + 0.5% Citric Acid twice at 7-day intervals.
- *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Boron and Copper in flooded rice soils of Telangana/AP are missing from reviewed extension advisories; consult local KVK before application.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Pests

### Cultural & Mechanical Protocols
- **Alternate Wetting and Drying (AWD):** Irrigate to 5 cm depth, allow water to subside naturally until field shows hairline cracks before re-irrigating (suppresses Brown Plant Hopper and saves 20–30% water).
- **Alleyways:** Form 30 cm wide alleyways every 2 meters during transplanting to allow air circulation and sunlight penetration (prevents BPH buildup).
- **Leaf Tip Clipping:** Clip top 1/3rd of seedling leaves before transplanting to destroy egg masses of Yellow Stem Borer.
- **Traps:** Install Pheromone Traps @ 5/acre for Yellow Stem Borer monitoring; install Light Traps @ 1/ha.

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Yellow Stem Borer (*Scirpophaga incertulas*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** "Dead hearts" (drying of central tiller shoot) at vegetative stage; "White ears" (chaffy erect white panicles) at flowering/heading stage.
- **Organic Option:** Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma japonicum* @ 1,00,000/ha (40,000/acre) 3 times at weekly intervals starting 30 DAT. Spray Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L) or Azadirachtin 1% @ 3 mL/L.
- **Chemical Option (Granular):** Apply Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 10 kg/ha (4 kg/acre) or Cartap Hydrochloride 4% GR @ 18.75 kg/ha in 2–3 cm standing water.
- **Chemical Option (Foliar Spray):** Spray Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 mL/L (60 mL/acre) or Flubendiamide 39.35% SC @ 0.2 mL/L (40 mL/acre) or Cartap Hydrochloride 50% SP @ 2 g/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** 14–20 days.

#### B. Brown Plant Hopper (BPH - *Nilaparvata lugens*) & White-Backed Plant Hopper (WBPH)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck sap from stem bases; plants turn yellow, dry up, and produce circular burnt patches known as "Hopper Burn".
- **Organic Option:** Drain water from the field for 3–4 days. Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) @ 50 g/L targeted strictly at the base of plants.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g formulation/ha** (40 g/acre) in 500–750 L water (0.2 g/L) at 50–55 DAT. Alternatively, spray Pymetrozine 50% WDG @ 0.6 g/L or Dinotefuran 20% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Triflumezopyrim 10% SC @ 0.4 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Thiamethoxam 25% WG: 14 days waiting period, 24-hour re-entry interval; Pymetrozine: 19 days waiting period.

#### C. Rice Leaf Folder (*Cnaphalocrocis medinalis*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Caterpillars roll leaf blades longitudinally and feed inside, scraping green chlorophyll leaving white longitudinal streaks.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Flubendiamide 20% WG @ 0.25 g/L or Cartap Hydrochloride 50% SP @ 2 g/L or Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2 mL/L.

#### D. Gall Midge (*Orseolia oryzae*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Apical meristem transformed into tubular "Silver Shoot" / "Onion Leaf" gall, preventing panicle formation.
- **Chemical Option:** Apply Fipronil 0.3% GR @ 15–20 kg/ha or Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2 mL/L at nursery / early tillering stage.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Rice Blast (*Magnaporthe oryzae* / *Pyricularia oryzae*)
- **Symptoms:** Spindle-shaped lesions with greyish-white centers and reddish-brown margins on leaves (Leaf Blast); dark brown to black rot at the panicle neck node causing panicle breakage and empty grains (Neck Blast).
- **Organic Option:** Spray *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/L (or 2.5 kg/ha) at tillering and panicle emergence stages.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Tricyclazole 75% WP @ 0.6 g/L** (120 g/acre) or Isoprothiolane 40% EC @ 1.5 mL/L or Kasugamycin 3% SL @ 2 mL/L at early lesion appearance or pre-heading stage.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Tricyclazole 75% WP requires a strict 28–30 day waiting period before harvest.

### B. Sheath Blight (*Rhizoctonia solani*)
- **Symptoms:** Oval or elliptical greenish-grey water-soaked lesions with brown borders on leaf sheaths near water line, coalescing to cover upper leaves.
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Trichoderma harzianum* / *viride* @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed with FYM at puddling.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 2 mL/L or Validamycin 3% L @ 2 mL/L or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1 mL/L or Thifluzamide 24% SC @ 0.75 mL/L.

### C. Bacterial Leaf Blight (BLB - *Xanthomonas oryzae* pv. *oryzae*)
- **Symptoms:** Linear translucent water-soaked streaks along leaf margins turning bright yellow and straw-colored with wavy margins; bacterial ooze droplets on leaves in morning.
- **Management:** Drain excess field water. Temporarily withhold Nitrogen top-dressing. Spray **Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L** twice at 10-day intervals. No complete chemical cure exists.

### D. Rice Tungro Virus (RTV)
- **Symptoms:** Stunted growth, orange-yellow leaf discoloration starting from leaf tips, reduced tillering. Vector: Green Leafhopper (*Nephotettix virescens*).
- **Management:** Rogue out infected hills. Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.2 g/L to control Green Leafhopper vectors.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - Harvest when **80–85% of grains in panicles turn straw-golden yellow** and upper panicle grains are hard.
  - Grain moisture content at harvest should ideally be **20–22%**.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Re-entry Safety:**
  - **Thiamethoxam 25% WG:** 14-day PHI waiting period; 24-hour re-entry safety interval.
  - **Tricyclazole 75% WP:** 28–30 day PHI waiting period.
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC:** 14–20 day PHI waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Neem, Trichoderma, Pseudomonas):** 0–1 day safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Handling:** Thresh harvested crop within 2–3 days. Shade dry or mechanically dry paddy grains to **12–14% moisture content** before long-term storage in Hermetic bags or gunny bags treated with Malathion 50% EC dust.

## 7. Cautions & Disclaimers

> [!WARNING]
> **CIBRC & Product Label Verification Mandatory:** Chemical pesticide dosages, formulations, water volumes, and PHIs cited from government and university handbooks must be confirmed against current CIBRC approved product labels before field application in Telangana (PJTSAU) and Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU).

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Missing-Dose Flag:** Regional foliar correction dosages for Boron and Copper in flooded rice soils, complete organic preparations for neck blast, and full PHI schedules for all fungicide combinations were omitted in open official sources and are explicitly flagged as missing rather than assumed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Bacterial & Viral Disease Control:** Chemical sprays do not cure Bacterial Leaf Blight or Rice Tungro Virus once established. Strict field sanitation, water drainage, and vector management are mandatory.
