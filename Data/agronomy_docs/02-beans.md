# Beans (French Bean / Common Bean / Chikkudu) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Phaseolus vulgaris* L. (French Bean / Common Bean / Bush & Pole Bean) & *Lablab purpureus* (Indian Bean / Chikkudu)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** ఫ్రెంచ్ బీన్స్ (French Beans), చిక్కుడుకాయ (Chikkudukaya), రాజ్మా (Rajma / Dry Bean)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Agronomic Management

### Soil & Field Preparation
- **Soil Requirement:** Well-drained sandy loam to clay loam soil rich in organic matter. Optimal pH range: 6.0 – 7.5.
- **Land Preparation:** Deep plowing 2–3 times followed by harrowing to achieve fine tilth. Incorporate **Farm Yard Manure (FYM)** or compost @ 25 tonnes/ha during final plowing.
- **Sowing Season:**
  - **Kharif:** June – July (onset of South-West monsoon)
  - **Rabi:** October – November (post-monsoon)
  - **Summer (Hills/Irrigated):** January – February

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Bush Bean Varieties (e.g., Arka Komal, Swarna Priya):** 50 – 55 kg/ha. Spacing: 45 cm (row-to-row) x 15 cm (plant-to-plant).
- **Pole Bean Varieties (e.g., Arka Sukanya, Kentucky Wonder):** 25 – 30 kg/ha. Spacing: 60 cm (row-to-row) x 15 cm (plant-to-plant). Provide bamboo staking support when plants reach 15–20 cm height.

### Bio-Fertilizer Seed Inoculation
- **Rhizobium Inoculation:** Treat seeds with *Rhizobium phaseoli* culture @ 200 g / 10 kg seed using rice kanji as binder to boost atmospheric nitrogen fixation.
- **Bio-Control Seed Protection:** Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed to prevent seed-borne wilt and root rot diseases.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Fertilizer Application Rates (NPK)
- **Recommended Dose:** 50 kg N : 75 kg P2O5 : 50 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Basal Application:** Apply 25 kg N, full 75 kg P2O5, and full 50 kg K2O at the time of sowing/land preparation.
- **Top Dressing:** Apply remaining 25 kg N at flowering stage (25–30 days after sowing) followed by earthing up and light irrigation.

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **FYM / Vermicompost:** FYM @ 25 t/ha or Vermicompost @ 2.5 t/ha.
- **Neem Cake:** Apply Neem Cake @ 1,250 kg/ha as soil amendment to reduce nematode and root grub damage.
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Soil application of *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/ha + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/ha mixed with 50 kg FYM.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Zinc Deficiency (Chlorosis in young leaves):** Soil application of Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) @ 25 kg/ha basal. Foliar spray of 0.5% ZnSO4 (5 g/L) + 0.25% Lime solution at vegetative stage.
- **Iron Deficiency (Interveinal chlorosis):** Foliar spray of Chelated Iron (Fe-EDTA) @ 1.5 g/L or Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 5 g/L.
- **Boron & Molybdenum Deficiency:** *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional dosages for Boron/Molybdenum foliar correction in Telangana/AP bean soils are missing from reviewed KVK bulletins; consult local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or Block Agriculture Officer before applying.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Protection

### Cultural & Physical Controls
- Install **Yellow Sticky Traps** @ 15/acre for whiteflies and aphids.
- Install **Blue Sticky Traps** @ 10/acre for thrips.
- Install **Pheromone Traps** @ 5/acre for *Helicoverpa armigera* and *Maruca vitrata* pod borers.
- Erect bird perches @ 10–15/acre to encourage predatory birds.

### Major Pests & Control Protocols

#### A. Aphids (*Aphis craccivora*) & Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Sucking sap from undersides of leaves, curling of leaf margins, honey-dew secretion leading to sooty mold, vectoring Bean Mosaic Virus.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L) or Azadirachtin 1% EC @ 2.5 mL/L + liquid soap (1 mL/L).
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.3 g/L.
- **PHI / Pre-Harvest Caution:** Mandatory 7–10 days wait before harvesting pods after chemical spray. *(Label confirmation required)*.

#### B. Thrips (*Thrips palmi*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Silvering of leaves, crinkling, malformed flower buds and pods.
- **Organic Option:** Spray NSKE 5% (Neem Seed Kernel Extract) or *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.5 mL/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L.

#### C. Spotted Pod Borer (*Maruca vitrata*) & Gram Pod Borer (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Webbing of leaves and flower buds, boreholes in pods with frass, feeding on seeds inside pods.
- **Biological Option:** Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* (Bt) @ 1 kg/ha or NPV (Nuclear Polyhedrosis Virus) @ 250 LE/ha at flowering stage.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 mL/L.
- **PHI:** Minimum 5–7 days Pre-Harvest Interval.

#### D. Stem Fly (*Ophiomyia phaseoli*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Swelling and cracking of stem at soil level, drying of seedlings in early establishment phase.
- **Chemical Option:** Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 70% WS @ 5 g/kg seed. Early prophylactic spray of Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L at 10–14 days after germination.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Powdery Mildew (*Erysiphe polygoni*)
- **Symptoms:** White powdery growth on leaves, stems, and pods leading to premature leaf fall and undersized pods.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Wettable Sulfur 80% WP @ 2 g/L or Neem Oil (5 mL/L) + Potassium Bicarbonate (5 g/L).
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 mL/L. Repeat after 12–15 days if necessary.

### B. Rust (*Uromyces appendiculatus*)
- **Symptoms:** Small reddish-brown pustules surrounded by yellow halos on lower leaf surfaces and pods.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Sulfur WP 80% @ 25 kg/ha dusting or foliar spray 2 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L.

### C. Anthracnose (*Colletotrichum lindemuthianum*)
- **Symptoms:** Dark brown sunken circular lesions on pods, stems, and leaves with pinkish spore masses in humid weather.
- **Seed Treatment:** Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2 g/kg seed.
- **Foliar Spray:** Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2 g/L at 10-day intervals.

### D. Root Rot & Wilt (*Rhizoctonia solani* / *Fusarium oxysporum*)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing of leaves, stunting, brown discoloration of vascular bundle at collar region, root decay.
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Trichoderma viride* / *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 5 kg/ha mixed in 500 kg compost.
- **Chemical Option:** Drench soil with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L at root zone.

### E. Bean Common Mosaic Virus (BCMV)
- **Symptoms:** Light and dark green mosaic pattern on leaves, downward cupping, puckering, stunting.
- **Management:** Vector control (Aphids). Uproot and burn virus-infected plants immediately. Use certified virus-free seeds. No curative chemical treatment exists.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Timing:** Pick green pods while they are tender, crisp, and seeds are not prominently bulged. Harvest every 3–4 days in early morning hours.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI):**
  - Chemical Fungicides (Mancozeb / Carbendazim): **7 – 10 days** minimum gap before harvest.
  - Insecticides (Emamectin Benzoate / Chlorantraniliprole): **5 – 7 days** minimum gap before harvest.
  - Bio-pesticides (Neem oil, Bt, Trichoderma): **0 – 1 day** safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Handling:** Keep harvested pods in shade, pack in perforated bags, and avoid water washing to prevent fungal rot during transport.
