# Muskmelon (Cucumis melo / Kharbooza) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Cucumis melo* L. (Muskmelon / Cantaloupe / Kharbooza)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** ఖర్బూజ (Kharbooza), కర్బూజ పండు (Karbhooja Pandu), దోసజాతి పండు (Cucurbit Fruit)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Land Preparation

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Well-drained fertile sandy loam to alluvial soils rich in organic matter. Sensitive to poorly drained heavy clay soils. Optimal soil pH: 6.0 – 7.0. Alkaline or saline soils cause poor seed germination and fruit cracking.
- **Sowing Season:**
  - **Main Season (Plains / Telangana & AP):** December – February (warm dry climate during fruit ripening enhances TSS/sweetness).
  - **Riverbed Cultivation:** November – December (along Godavari, Krishna, and Pennar river basins).

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Seed Rate:** 1.5 – 2.0 kg/ha for open-pollinated varieties; 800 g – 1.0 kg/ha for F1 Hybrids.
- **Spacing:**
  - **Channel System:** 1.5 m to 2.0 m between channels x 60 cm between plants along the channel edge.
  - **Drip & Silver-Black Mulch System:** Raised beds of 1.2 m width, 30 cm height; 2.0 m row-to-row spacing x 45–60 cm plant-to-plant spacing.

### Seed Treatment & Inoculation
- **Bio-Control Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed to protect against soil-borne damping-off and Fusarium wilt.
- **Chemical Seed Treatment:** Soak seeds in *Carbendazim 50% WP* @ 2 g/kg seed or *Thiram 75% WS* @ 2.5 g/kg seed in 1 liter of water for 30 minutes before sowing.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Recommended NPK Fertilizer Rates
- **Total Fertilizer Requirement:** 80 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 60 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Basal Soil Application:** Apply 40 kg N (half dose), full 60 kg P2O5, and 30 kg K2O (half dose) at the time of final land preparation/bed formation.
- **Top Dressing / Fertigation:**
  - Apply remaining 40 kg N and 30 kg K2O at vine elongation/vining stage (30 days after sowing - DAS).
  - For drip-fertigation: Inject 19-19-19 soluble NPK @ 5 kg/ha weekly during vegetative growth, switching to 13-0-45 (Potassium Nitrate) @ 5 kg/ha weekly during fruit development to increase Brix/sweetness.

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Apply FYM / Compost @ 20 – 25 tonnes/ha during land preparation.
- **Neem Cake:** Apply Neem Cake @ 500 kg/ha to control soil grubs, nematodes, and root-knot diseases.
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Soil application of *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/ha + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/ha mixed in 100 kg compost.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Boron Deficiency (Fruit cracking, hollow heart, poor seed set):** Soil application of Borax @ 10 kg/ha basal. Foliar spray of Solubor (Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate) @ 1.0 – 1.5 g/L at flowering initiation.
- **Calcium Deficiency (Blossom end rot, soft fruit tip):** Foliar spray of Calcium Nitrate @ 5 g/L at early fruit set stage.
- **Zinc & Iron Deficiency:** Foliar spray of Chelated Zinc (Zn-EDTA) @ 1 g/L or Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 3 g/L.  
  *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Magnesium and Copper in AP/Telangana muskmelon soils are missing from reviewed extension bulletins; consult local KVK before applying.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Biological Controls

### Cultural & Mechanical Protocols
- **Soil Solarization:** Cover nursery beds with transparent polyethylene sheet (25 micron) for 30 days during May–June to eradicate soil-borne fungi and weed seeds.
- **Mulching:** Use 25-micron Silver-Black Polyethylene Mulch film to suppress weeds, retain moisture, and deter sap-sucking pests.
- **Fruit Fly Traps:** Install **Cue-lure / MAT (Male Annihilation Technique) Traps** @ 4–5 traps/acre to trap male Melon Fruit Flies (*Bacterocera cucurbitae*).
- **Yellow Sticky Traps:** Install Yellow Sticky Traps @ 15/acre to catch whiteflies and leaf miners.

### Major Pest Management

#### A. Melon Fruit Fly (*Bacterocera cucurbitae*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Female fly punctures young fruits and deposits eggs inside. Maggots feed on internal pulp, causing fruit rotting, resin leakage, and premature drop.
- **Organic Option:** Collect and destroy all infested dropped fruits daily. Hang Cue-lure wood block traps @ 5/acre. Spray Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L).
- **Chemical Option:** Poison bait spray — Mix 20 mL Malathion 50% EC + 200 g Jaggery in 20 Liters of water and spray as coarse drops on lower leaves. Alternatively, spray Spinosad 45% SC @ 0.3 mL/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 1.7 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Minimum 7 days after insecticide spray. *(Label confirmation required)*.

#### B. Red Pumpkin Beetle (*Aulacophora foveicollis*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Orange-red adult beetles defoliate cotyledons and young leaves by biting circular holes. Grubs feed on roots under soil.
- **Organic Option:** Dust wood ash on cotyledon leaves early morning. Hand-pick adult beetles.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Carbaryl 50% WP @ 2 g/L or Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 2 mL/L at seedling stage (first 20 days).

#### C. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*) & Aphids (*Aphis gossypii*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Sap sucking from lower leaf surfaces, leaf curling, honey-dew secretion, vectoring Cucurbit Yellow Stunting Disorder Virus (CYSDV).
- **Organic Option:** Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) @ 50 g/L or Azadirachtin 1% EC @ 2.5 mL/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L or Acetamiprid 20% SP @ 0.2 g/L.

#### D. Serpentile Leaf Miner (*Liriomyza trifolii*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Serpentine white winding mines on leaves, reducing photosynthetic area.
- **Management:** Remove badly mined leaves. Spray Abamectin 1.8% EC @ 0.5 mL/L or Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L).

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Downy Mildew (*Pseudoperonospora cubensis*)
- **Symptoms:** Angular yellow lesions bounded by leaf veins on upper leaf surface; purplish/grey downy fungal growth under leaves during wet humid conditions.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Bordeaux Mixture 1%.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L or Fosetyl-Al 80% WP @ 2 g/L.

### B. Powdery Mildew (*Erysiphe cichoracearum* / *Podosphaera xanthii*)
- **Symptoms:** White powdery talc-like circular spots on upper and lower leaf surfaces, petioles, and young stems under dry warm weather.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Wettable Sulfur 80% WP @ 2 g/L or Potassium Bicarbonate @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Karathane / Dinocap 48% EC @ 1 mL/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 mL/L or Hexaconazole 5% EC @ 1 mL/L.

### C. Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *melo*)
- **Symptoms:** Sudden wilting of vines, starting from older leaves; brown vascular discoloration in root and stem bases.
- **Biological Option:** Soil drenching with *Trichoderma viride* @ 5 g/L + *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Soil drenching with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L around root zone.

### D. Cucumber Mosaic Virus (CMV) & Zucchini Yellow Mosaic Virus (ZYMV)
- **Symptoms:** Severe leaf mottling, blistering, shoe-stringing, stunting, bumpy distorted fruits.
- **Management:** Vector control (Whitefly/Aphid management with Neem oil/Imidacloprid). Rogue out infected plants. No chemical cure exists.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - **Full-Slip Stage:** Fruit easily separates from the vine stem with slight thumb pressure leaving a clean smooth scar (ideal for local market).
  - **Half-Slip Stage:** Stem requires moderate pulling force to detach (ideal for long-distance transport).
  - **Netting & Color:** Skin background color turns from green to creamy yellowish; surface netting becomes raised and corky.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI):**
  - Chemical Insecticides (Malathion / Dimethoate / Spinosad): **5 – 7 days** minimum safety wait.
  - Chemical Fungicides (Cymoxanil / Mancozeb / Metalaxyl): **7 – 10 days** safety wait.
  - Bio-pesticides (Neem Oil, Trichoderma): **0 – 1 day** safety margin.
- **Harvesting Caution:** Harvest early morning using sharp secateurs. Do not harvest immediately after irrigation to prevent water accumulation and melon decay.

> [!IMPORTANT]
