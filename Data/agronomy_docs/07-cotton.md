# Cotton (Gossypium hirsutum / పత్తి) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Gossypium hirsutum* L. / *Gossypium arboreum* L. (Cotton / పత్తి)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** పత్తి (Patti / Cotton), పత్తి చేను (Patti Chenu / Cotton Field), పత్తి కాయ (Patti Kaya / Cotton Boll)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Land Preparation

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Deep black cotton soils (Vertisols) with high clay content and water-retention capacity, or deep well-drained red loamy soils. Sensitive to waterlogging and severe salinity. Optimal soil pH: 6.0 – 8.0.
- **Sowing Season:**
  - **Kharif Season (Main Season):** June – July (with onset of South-West Monsoon). Early sowing in June reduces Pink Bollworm infestation risks.

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Bt Cotton Hybrids:** 1.5 – 2.0 kg/ha (approx. 450 g packet/acre x 2 packets including non-Bt refuge seed).
- **Non-Bt Hybrids / Varieties:** 4.0 – 5.0 kg/ha (1.5 – 2.0 kg/acre).
- **High Density Planting System (HDPS - Straight Varieties):** 10 – 12 kg/ha (4 – 5 kg/acre).
- **Spacing:**
  - Irrigated Bt Hybrids: 90 cm row-to-row x 60 cm plant-to-plant or 120 cm x 45 cm.
  - Rainfed Black Soils: 90 cm x 45 cm or 75 cm x 30 cm.
  - HDPS Cotton: 60 cm x 15 cm or 45 cm x 15 cm (Plant Density: ~1,11,111 plants/ha).

### Seed Treatment & Inoculation
- **Sucking Pest Protection:** Delinted seeds treated with *Imidacloprid 70% WS* @ 7 g/kg seed or *Thiamethoxam 30 FS* @ 10 mL/kg seed to protect against Jassids, Thrips, and Aphids for the first 30 days.
- **Bio-Control Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed.
- **Fungicidal Treatment:** Treat with *Carboxin 37.5% + Thiram 37.5% DS* @ 3 g/kg seed or *Carbendazim 50% WP* @ 2 g/kg seed to prevent seedling blight and root rot.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Recommended NPK Fertilizer Rates
- **Rainfed Cotton (Telangana & AP):** 90 kg N : 45 kg P2O5 : 45 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Irrigated Bt Cotton Hybrids:** 120–150 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 60 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Application Schedule:**
  - **Basal Application:** Apply 20% of Nitrogen (24 kg/ha), full dose of P2O5 (60 kg/ha), and 20% of K2O (12 kg/ha) at sowing.
  - **1st Top Dressing (Squaring Stage - 30 to 35 DAS):** Apply 40% of Nitrogen (48 kg/ha) + 40% of K2O (24 kg/ha).
  - **2nd Top Dressing (Peak Flowering Stage - 60 to 65 DAS):** Apply remaining 40% of Nitrogen (48 kg/ha) + remaining 40% of K2O (24 kg/ha).

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Apply FYM / Compost @ 10 – 15 tonnes/ha during land preparation.
- **Zinc & Soil Health:** Apply Zinc Sulphate Heptahydrate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 50 kg/ha (20 kg/acre) basal in Zn-deficient black soils.
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Soil application of *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/acre + PSB @ 2 kg/acre mixed in 100 kg compost.

### Micronutrient Management & Leaf Reddening ("Patti Errapaduta")
- **Magnesium Deficiency & Leaf Reddening ("Patti Errapaduta"):**
  - **Symptoms:** Interveinal chlorosis turning purplish-red on mature leaves while veins remain green; premature defoliation and boll shedding.
  - **Foliar Correction:** Spray **Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) 1.0% (10 g/L) + Urea 1.0% (10 g/L)** or 2.0% KNO3 (Potassium Nitrate 20 g/L) at 45, 60, 75, and 90 DAS.
- **Boron Deficiency (Boll Cracking & Petiole Ringing):**
  - **Soil Application:** Apply Borax @ 10 kg/ha basal.
  - **Foliar Correction:** Spray Solubor (Disodium Octaborate Tetrahydrate) @ 1.5 g/L at flowering/boll development.
- *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Copper and Manganese in saline cotton soils of Telangana/AP are missing from reviewed advisories; consult local KVK before application.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Economic Threshold Levels (ETL)

### Economic Threshold Levels (ETL)
- **Pink Bollworm:** 10% damaged fruiting parts / bolls or 8 male moths/trap/night for 3 consecutive nights.
- **Helicoverpa / American Bollworm:** 1 egg or 1 larva per plant or 5–10% square damage.
- **Jassids / Leafhoppers:** 50 jassids / 50 leaves or 1–2 jassids/leaf (marginal leaf yellowing/cupping).
- **Whiteflies:** 5–10 whiteflies per leaf or honey-dew soot mold appearance.
- **Aphids:** 15% infested plants.

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Pink Bollworm (*Pectinophora gossypiella*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** "Rosetted flowers" (petals tied together with webbing), small unnoticeable entrance holes on bolls, internal lint staining, and eaten seed kernels.
- **Pheromone & Light Traps:** Install Pink Bollworm Pheromone Traps @ 5–8 traps/acre at 45 DAS. Hang PBW PB-Rope / Mating Disruption ropes.
- **Biological Option:** Release egg parasitoid *Trichogrammatoidea bactrae* @ 1,00,000/ha at 45 & 60 DAS. Spray *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Profenofos 50% EC @ 2 mL/L** or Indoxacarb 14.5% SC @ 1 mL/L or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L or Thiodicarb 75% WP @ 2 g/L during peak square to early boll formation stage (60–90 DAS).
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Profenofos 50% EC: 30 days waiting period; Indoxacarb: 14 days; Emamectin Benzoate: 14 days.

#### B. Jassids / Leafhoppers (*Amrasca biguttula biguttula*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck cell sap from lower leaf surface; leaf margins turn yellow, curl downwards, turn brick-red, and dry up ("Hopper Burn").
- **Organic Option:** Spray Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L) or NSKE 5% (50 g/L) or *Verticillium lecanii* @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 100 g formulation/ha** (40 g/acre in 500–750 L water / 0.2 g/L) at 25–30 DAS or at first pest appearance. Alternatively, spray Flonicamid 50% WG @ 0.3 g/L or Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Thiamethoxam 25% WG: 21 days waiting period, 24-hour re-entry safety interval.

#### C. Whitefly (*Bemisia tabaci*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Sap sucking, sticky honeydew drops, black soot mold on lower leaves, vectoring Cotton Leaf Curl Virus (CLCuV).
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Diafenthiuron 50% WP @ 1.25 g/L or Pyriproxyfen 10% EC @ 2 mL/L or Spiromesifen 22.9% SC @ 1 mL/L or Afidopyropen 50 g/L DC @ 2 mL/L.

#### D. Spotted Bollworm (*Earias vitella*) & American Bollworm (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Bores into growing terminal shoots causing shoot wilting ("top drying"), bores into squares and bolls with frass outside.
- **Biological Option:** Spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/acre or Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.3 mL/L.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Bacterial Leaf Blight / Black Arm (*Xanthomonas citri* pv. *malvacearum*)
- **Symptoms:** Angular water-soaked lesions on leaves bounded by veins; black elongated lesions on petioles and stems ("Black Arm"); circular sunken black spots on bolls ("Boll Rot").
- **Management:** Seed treatment with Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L. Spray **Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L + Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L** twice at 12-day intervals at symptom onset.

### B. Alternaria Leaf Spot (*Alternaria macrospora*)
- **Symptoms:** Small, circular brown spots with concentric rings and purple margins on leaves, causing premature defoliation.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L.

### C. Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *vasinfectum*) & Damping-off
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing and browning of leaf margins, yellowing spreading inwards, vascular dark brown discoloration of stem xylem, plant wilting.
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Trichoderma viride* @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed in FYM at sowing.
- **Chemical Option:** Soil drenching with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride @ 2.5 g/L around root zone.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvesting Guidance ("Clean Picking"):**
  - Pick seed cotton when bolls are **fully open and dry**.
  - Pick during early morning hours after dew dries up to avoid leaf trash contamination.
  - Separate stained, insect-damaged, or immature cotton from clean white locks.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Safety Wait:**
  - **Thiamethoxam 25% WG:** 21-day PHI waiting period; 24-hour re-entry safety interval.
  - **Profenofos 50% EC:** 30-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Flonicamid 50% WG / Indoxacarb 14.5% SC:** 14-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Neem, Trichoderma, Beauveria):** 0–1 day safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Storage:** Dry picked seed cotton to **below 8% moisture content** before storing in clean, dry, ventilated godowns away from chemicals.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pink Bollworm Resistance Management:** Avoid synthetic pyrethroid sprays early in the season. Use Pheromone traps and light traps strictly based on ETL thresholds.
