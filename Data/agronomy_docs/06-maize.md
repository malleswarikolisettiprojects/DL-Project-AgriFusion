# Maize (Zea mays / మొక్కజొన్న) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (PJTSAU Advisory Zone) & Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU Advisory Zone), India  
**Crop Species:** *Zea mays* L. (Maize / Corn / మొక్కజొన్న)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** మొక్కజొన్న (Mokkajonna / Maize), జొన్న కంకి (Jonna Kanki / Maize Cob), జొన్న పైరు (Jonna Pairu / Maize Crop)

---

## 1. Crop Establishment & Land Preparation

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Well-drained fertile loams, silt loams, and deep alluvial soils with good organic matter and high water-holding capacity. Highly sensitive to waterlogging. Optimal soil pH: 6.5 – 7.5.
- **Sowing Seasons:**
  - **Kharif Season:** June – July (with onset of monsoon).
  - **Rabi Season (Main Commercial Season in AP/Telangana):** October – November.
  - **Spring / Summer Season:** January – February.

### Seed Rate & Spacing
- **Grain Maize Hybrids:** 8 – 10 kg/acre (20 kg/ha).
- **Sweet Corn:** 3 – 4 kg/acre (8–10 kg/ha).
- **Baby Corn:** 10 – 12 kg/acre (25 kg/ha).
- **Fodder Maize:** 15 – 20 kg/acre (40–50 kg/ha).
- **Spacing:**
  - Grain Hybrids: 60 cm row-to-row x 20 cm plant-to-plant (Plant Density: ~75,000 plants/ha).
  - Sweet Corn: 60 cm x 25 cm.
  - Baby Corn: 45 cm x 20 cm (higher plant population).

### Seed Treatment & Inoculation
- **Bio-Control Seed Treatment:** Treat seeds with *Trichoderma viride* @ 4 g/kg seed or *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/kg seed to protect against soil-borne damping-off and charcoal rot.
- **Sucking Pest Protection:** Treat seeds with *Imidacloprid 600 FS* @ 6 mL/kg seed or *Thiamethoxam 30 FS* @ 8 mL/kg seed to protect young seedlings from Shoot Fly and Aphids for the first 20 days.
- **Downy Mildew Protection:** Soak seeds in *Metalaxyl 35% WS* @ 3 g/kg seed or *Thiram 75% WS* @ 3 g/kg seed.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Recommended NPK Fertilizer Rates
- **Irrigated Hybrid Maize (Telangana & AP):** 120–150 kg N : 60 kg P2O5 : 40–50 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Rainfed Maize:** 80–100 kg N : 40 kg P2O5 : 30 kg K2O per hectare.
- **Application Schedule:**
  - **Basal Application:** Apply 25% of Nitrogen (30–35 kg/ha), full dose of P2O5 (60 kg/ha), and 50% of K2O (20–25 kg/ha) at the time of land preparation / sowing band.
  - **1st Top Dressing (Knee-High Stage - 25 to 30 DAS):** Apply 50% of Nitrogen (60–75 kg/ha).
  - **2nd Top Dressing (Tasseling / Silking Stage - 45 to 50 DAS):** Apply remaining 25% of Nitrogen (30–35 kg/ha) and remaining 50% of K2O (20–25 kg/ha).

### Organic & Bio-Inputs
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Apply FYM / Compost @ 10 – 12 tonnes/ha during final land preparation.
- **Neem Cake:** Apply Neem Cake @ 250 kg/ha to control soil grubs and cutworms.
- **Bio-fertilizers:** Soil application of *Azospirillum* @ 2 kg/acre + Phosphate Solubilizing Bacteria (PSB) @ 2 kg/acre mixed in 100 kg compost/FYM.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Zinc Deficiency ("White Bud" of Maize):**
  - **Symptoms:** White or light yellow chlorotic bands on either side of the midrib on young expanding leaves; stunted growth and delayed flowering.
  - **Soil Application (Basal):** Apply Zinc Sulphate Heptahydrate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 25 kg/ha (10 kg/acre) or Zinc Sulphate Monohydrate (33%) @ 15 kg/ha at sowing.
  - **Foliar Correction:** Spray 0.5% Zinc Sulphate (5 g/L) + 1.0% Urea (10 g/L) twice at 20 & 35 DAS.
- **Iron Deficiency (Interveinal Chlorosis):** Foliar spray of 1.0% Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4 10 g/L) + 0.5% Citric Acid twice at 7-day intervals.
- **Magnesium Deficiency (Interveinal Stripe Chlorosis on Older Leaves):** Apply Magnesium Sulphate @ 20 kg/ha basal.
- *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Copper and Manganese in maize soils of Telangana/AP are missing from reviewed extension advisories; consult local KVK before application.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Pests

### Cultural & Mechanical Protocols
- **Whorl Sanding / Ashing:** Apply 10 g fine sand or wood ash mixed with Neem cake (9:1) into leaf whorls at 15–20 DAS to suppress Fall Armyworm larvae mechanically.
- **Intercropping:** Intercrop Maize with Cowpea or Blackgram in a 2:1 row ratio to reduce Fall Armyworm and Stem Borer incidence.
- **Pheromone Traps:** Install FAW Pheromone Traps @ 5–8 traps/acre for monitoring and mass trapping male moths.

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Fall Armyworm (FAW - *Spodoptera frugiperda*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Pin-hole lesions on young leaves, ragged leaf margins, heavy sawdust-like frass accumulation inside central leaf whorls, destroyed growing tips ("dead heart").
- **Organic Option:** Spray *Bacillus thuringiensis* var. *kurstaki* (Bt) @ 2 g/L or *Metarhizium anisopliae* / *Nomuraea rileyi* @ 5 g/L or Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L). Hand-pick egg masses and destroy.
- **Chemical Option (First Window - Early Whorl / 15–20 DAS):** Spray **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L** (80 g/acre in 200 L water) directed into leaf whorls.
- **Chemical Option (Second Window - Mid Whorl / 30–35 DAS):** Spray **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC @ 0.4 mL/L** (80 mL/acre) or Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Emamectin Benzoate: 14 days waiting period; Chlorantraniliprole: 14 days.

#### B. Maize Stem Borer (*Chilo partellus*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Parallel "shot holes" in emerging leaves, drying of central shoots ("dead hearts") in 2–4 week old seedlings.
- **Biological Option:** Release egg parasitoid *Trichogramma chilonis* @ 1,00,000/ha at 10 & 20 DAS.
- **Chemical Option (Granular Whorl Application):** Apply Carbofuran 3% CG @ 10 kg/ha or Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR @ 10 kg/ha (4 kg/acre) into leaf whorls at 20 DAS.

#### C. Shoot Fly (*Atherigona soccata*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Attacks 1–4 week old seedlings causing yellowing and dead hearts.
- **Management:** Seed treatment with Imidacloprid 600 FS @ 6 mL/kg seed. Spray Dimethoate 30% EC @ 2 mL/L at 7–10 DAS.

#### D. Corn Cob Borer / Helicoverpa (*Helicoverpa armigera*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Larvae feed on silks and bore into tip of developing cobs, promoting fungal cob rot.
- **Management:** Spray HaNPV @ 250 LE/acre or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L at silk emergence stage.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Turcicum Leaf Blight (TLB - *Exserohilum turcicum*)
- **Symptoms:** Long, elliptical greyish-green or tan lesions (2.5 – 15 cm) on leaves starting from lower leaves and spreading upwards during cool humid weather.
- **Organic Option:** Spray *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L** or Zineb 75% WP @ 2 g/L or Azoxystrobin 18.2% + Difenoconazole 11.4% SC @ 1 mL/L at symptom onset. Repeat after 12 days.

### B. Maydis Leaf Blight (*Bipolaris maydis*)
- **Symptoms:** Small, rectangular tan lesions with reddish-brown borders between leaf veins.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L.

### C. Charcoal Rot / Post- Flowering Stalk Rot (*Macrophomina phaseolina* / *Fusarium verticillioides*)
- **Symptoms:** Premature leaf drying, softening of stalk lower internodes, internal spongy disintegration with dark grey micro-sclerotia inside pith.
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Trichoderma viride* @ 2.5 kg/ha mixed in FYM at sowing.
- **Chemical Option:** Soil drenching with Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L around root zone. Ensure adequate moisture during post-flowering stage.

### D. Sorghum Downy Mildew (*Peronosclerospora sorghi*)
- **Symptoms:** Pale yellow longitudinal chlorotic stripes on leaves, white cottony downy growth on under-surface under cool moist morning conditions, bushy stunted plants ("Crazy Top").
- **Management:** Seed treatment with Metalaxyl 35% WS @ 3 g/kg seed. Spray Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L at 20 DAS.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - **Grain Maize:** Harvest when outer sheath wrapper leaves turn pale yellow/straw brown, cob grains become hard and shiny, and a dark black layer forms at the kernel base (grain moisture ~20–22%).
  - **Sweet Corn:** Harvest at milk stage (70–75 DAS) when silks turn dark brown and kernels emit milky fluid when punctured.
  - **Baby Corn:** Harvest 2–3 days after silk emergence before pollination occurs.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Safety Wait:**
  - **Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG:** 14-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Chlorantraniliprole 18.5% SC:** 14-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Propiconazole / Mancozeb:** 15–20 day PHI waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Bt, Metarhizium, Trichoderma):** 0–1 day safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Handling & Aflatoxin Prevention:** Shell harvested cobs and sun-dry grains down to **12% moisture content** before storage to prevent *Aspergillus flavus* contamination and toxic Aflatoxin production.

## 7. Cautions & Disclaimers

> [!WARNING]
> **CIBRC & Product Label Verification Mandatory:** Pesticide dosages, formulations, water volumes, and PHIs cited from government handbooks must be confirmed against current CIBRC approved product labels before field application in Telangana (PJTSAU) and Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU).

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Missing-Dose Flag:** Universal PHIs for all maize pesticide formulations and regional foliar correction doses for Copper and Manganese in maize soils were omitted in reviewed open official sources and are explicitly flagged as missing rather than assumed.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aflatoxin Prevention:** Moisture control during post-harvest drying is critical. High grain moisture (>14%) promotes *Aspergillus flavus* mold and fatal aflatoxin accumulation.
