## Structured Scheme Entries

### 1. Rythu Bharosa — Telangana Agriculture Investment Support Scheme
- **Purpose:** Telangana flagship initiative providing financial assistance for agricultural inputs at the beginning of each crop cycle (replaces former Rythu Bandhu).
- **Benefits:** **₹12,000 per acre per year** paid in two equal seasonal instalments of **₹6,000 per acre** for Kharif (Rainy season) and Rabi (Winter season). Includes a **₹500 per quintal paddy crop bonus**. Landless agricultural labourers receive ₹12,000/year under the linked Indiramma Atmiya Bharosa programme.
- **Eligibility:** Active arable farming plots verified via satellite mapping and registered in **Bhu Bharati** revenue portal; ROFR (Recognition of Forest Rights) landholders; registered tenant farmers with valid lease agreements. Excludes real-estate plots and non-arable fallow lands.
- **Application & Route:** Managed via Telangana Agriculture Department (AEO / MAO / ADA / DAO officers) and Bhu Bharati portal records.

### 2. Rythu Bima — Telangana Farmers Group Life Insurance Scheme
- **Eligibility:** All enrolled farmers aged **18 to 59 years**.
- **Benefit:** Insured amount of **₹5.00 Lakh** deposited directly into the designated nominee bank account within **10 days** of the farmer's death due to any cause (including natural death).
- **Premium:** 100% of the insurance premium is paid by the Government of Telangana to Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC).
- **Claim Route:** Village-level Agriculture Extension Officers (AEOs) collect death data and submit claims via NIC portal directly to LIC on behalf of the nominee. The nominee does NOT need to visit any LIC office.

### 3. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi in Telangana)
- **Benefit:** ₹6,000/year in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000.
- **Eligibility:** Landholding in farmer's own name in Bhu Bharati land records. Requires Aadhaar authentication & mandatory e-KYC.

### 4. PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana in Telangana)
- **Coverage:** Notified crops in notified mandals/villages. Crop loan holders (KCC) and non-loanee farmers.
- **Helpline & Portal:** 14447 helpline & [pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in/).
