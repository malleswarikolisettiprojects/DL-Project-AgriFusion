# Grapes (Vitis vinifera / ద్రాక్ష / अंगूर) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (Rangareddy, Medchal, Sangareddy districts - PJTSAU) & Andhra Pradesh (Anantapur, Kurnool districts - ANGRAU), India  
**Crop Species:** *Vitis vinifera* L. (Grapes / ద్రాక్ష)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu/Hindi):** ద్రాక్ష (Draksha / Grapes), ద్రాక్ష తోట (Draksha Thota / Grape Vineyard), अंगूर (Angoor)

---

## 1. Vineyard Establishment & Pruning Systems

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Deep, well-drained sandy loams, red loams, and light alluvial soils with good organic matter. Highly sensitive to waterlogging and salinity. Optimal soil pH: 6.5 – 7.5. Electrical Conductivity (EC) must be < 1.5 dS/m.
- **Training System:** Bower System (Pandal System) or Y-Trellis / Extended Y-Trellis System (for optimum canopy management, aeration, and disease control).
- **Pruning System (Two Pruning System in South India):**
  - **April Pruning (Foundation / Summer Pruning):** Performed in April after harvest. Shoots pruned back to 1–2 buds (cane formation spurs) to encourage vigorous vegetative growth during monsoon.
  - **October Pruning (Forward / Winter Pruning):** Performed in October. Canes pruned back to 5–10 buds (fruiting spurs) to induce flower buds, fruiting, and bunch development.

### Spacing & Rootstocks
- **Spacing:** 3.0 m row-to-row x 1.5 m plant-to-plant or 2.7 m x 1.8 m (Density: ~2,200 – 2,500 vines/ha).
- **Salinity & Drought Rootstocks:** Dogridge (*Vitis champini*) or Salt Creek rootstocks recommended for drought and salinity resistance in AP/Telangana soils.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Annual NPK Fertilizer Rates per Bearing Vine (> 5 Years Old)
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** 50 – 60 kg/vine/year (up to 100 kg/vine for heavy bearing cultivars like Thompson Seedless / Anab-e-Shahi) applied post-April pruning.
- **Annual NPK Fertilizer Dosage per Vine:**
  - **Post-April Pruning (Foundation Phase):** 300 g N : 200 g P2O5 : 200 g K2O per vine.
  - **Post-October Pruning (Forward Phase):** 200 g N : 300 g P2O5 : 600 g K2O per vine.
  - **Total Annual Dose per Vine:** ~500 g N : 500 g P2O5 : 800 g K2O per vine.

### Plant Growth Regulators (PGR) for Berry Quality
- **Gibberellic Acid (GA3) Application Schedule:**
  - **Bunch Elongation:** Spray GA3 @ 10–15 ppm (10–15 mg/L) at 10–12 days post-October pruning (5–6 leaf stage).
  - **Berry Thinning:** Spray GA3 @ 20–30 ppm at 40–50% flowering.
  - **Berry Enlargement:** Spray GA3 @ 40–50 ppm twice when berries reach 4–6 mm diameter size.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Zinc & Boron Deficiency ("Shot Berries" / "Hen and Chick" Disorder):**
  - **Symptoms:** Uneven berry size, small seedless shot berries mixed with normal berries, flower cluster drop, poor pollination.
  - **Soil Application:** Apply Zinc Sulphate @ 25 kg/ha + Borax @ 10 kg/ha post-April pruning.
  - **Foliar Spray:** Spray **Boric Acid @ 0.1% (1 g/L) + Zinc Sulphate @ 0.2% (2 g/L) + Urea @ 0.5% (5 g/L)** at pre-bloom stage (15 days post-October pruning) and 50% bloom stage.
- **Iron Chlorosis (Calcareous Soil Yellowing):** Foliar spray of Chelated Iron (Fe-EDTA) @ 1.5 g/L or Ferrous Sulphate @ 3 g/L at active vegetative stage.
- *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Copper and Manganese in grape vineyards of Telangana/AP are missing from reviewed extension advisories; consult local KVK before application.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Pests

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Grape Mealybug (*Maconellicoccus hirsutus*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck sap from canes, leaves, shoots, flower bunches, and berries. White waxy cottony masses cover bunch stems; honeydew secretion attracts black soot mold making berries sticky and unmarketable.
- **Biological Option:** Release Australian Ladybird Beetle (*Cryptolaemus montrouzieri*) @ 1,500 – 2,000 beetles/ha (500–750/acre) at 30 days post-October pruning.
- **Organic Option:** Scrap off loose bark post-April pruning. Spray Fish Oil Rosin Soap (FORS) @ 25 g/L or Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L).
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Buprofezin 25% SC @ 1.25 mL/L** or Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.25 g/L targeted at trunk bases, canes, and berry clusters.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Buprofezin 25% SC: 42 days waiting period; Imidacloprid 17.8% SL: 60 days.

#### B. Grape Thrips (*Scirtothrips dorsalis*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Scab-like corky brown scarring on berry surface, leaf curling upwards, blossom drop, berry cracking.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 mL/L** or Fipronil 5% SC @ 1.0 mL/L or Emamectin Benzoate 5% SG @ 0.4 g/L during flowering and early berry set stage.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Spinetoram: 30 days waiting period; Fipronil: 60 days.

#### C. Grape Flea Beetle (*Scelodonta strigicollis*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Adult beetles scrape and bore into emerging buds immediately post-pruning, destroying buds ("Bud destruction").
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Phosalone 35% EC @ 2 mL/L or Quinalphos 25% EC @ 2 mL/L immediately post-pruning at bud burst.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### 1% Bordeaux Mixture Preparation Protocol
- **Preparation Method:** Dissolve 1.0 kg Copper Sulphate (Blue Vitriol) in 50 L water in a plastic container. Dissolve 1.0 kg Quick Lime (Calcium Oxide) in 50 L water in a separate container. Pour the Copper Sulphate solution slowly into the Lime solution while stirring continuously. Test with a shiny iron nail (if copper coats nail, add more lime until neutral pH 7.0). Use fresh on the same day.

### A. Downy Mildew (*Plasmopara viticola*)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowish translucent oil spots on upper leaf surface; white dense cottony fungal growth on lower leaf surface under high humidity; brown dried shriveled berries ("Leather berries").
- **Organic / Preventive Option:** Spray **1% Bordeaux Mixture** or Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Potassium Phosphonate 40% @ 3 mL/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Cymoxanil 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2 g/L** or Dimethomorph 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 mL/L or Mandipropamid 23.4% SC @ 0.8 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Dimethomorph 50% WP: 66 days waiting period; Azoxystrobin: 30 days; Mandipropamid: 30 days.

### B. Powdery Mildew (*Uncinula necator* / *Erysiphe necator*)
- **Symptoms:** White powdery talc-like patches on leaves, canes, flower clusters, and berries; berries crack open exposing seeds, fail to mature.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Wettable Sulfur 80% WP @ 3 g/L or Potassium Bicarbonate @ 5 g/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Myclobutanil 10% WP @ 0.4 g/L** or Penconazole 10% EC @ 0.5 mL/L or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 0.5 mL/L or Tetraconazole 3.8% EW @ 0.75 mL/L.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Myclobutanil: 30 days waiting period; Penconazole: 30 days.

### C. Anthracnose / Bird's Eye Spot (*Elsinoe ampelina*)
- **Symptoms:** Small dark brown sunken spots with grey centers and dark reddish margins on leaves and berries ("Bird's Eye" spots); dark sunken cankers on young canes.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Carbendazim 50% WP @ 1 g/L or Copper Hydroxide 77% WP @ 2 g/L or Chlorothalonil 75% WP @ 2 g/L.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - Harvest when berries reach full variety-specific color, Total Soluble Solids (TSS) reach **18–20° Brix** (measured using a hand refractometer), and bunch stem pedicel turns brownish-woody.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Safety Wait:**
  - **Captan 50% WP:** PHI listed as dash (-) in PPQS table *(Explicit missing PHI flag; observe safety wait before harvest)*.
  - **Dimethomorph 50% WP:** 66-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Fipronil 5% SC / Imidacloprid 17.8% SL:** 60-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Buprofezin 25% SC:** 42-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Spinetoram 11.7% SC / Azoxystrobin 23% SC:** 30-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Cryptolaemus, Neem, Sulfur):** 0–3 days safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Handling & Cold Chain:** Clip bunches with secateurs leaving a 2 cm peduncle. Pre-cool bunches at 4°C within 4 hours of harvest; pack in dual-release SO2 generator pads (Grape Guard sheets) and store at 0–1°C and 90–95% Relative Humidity.

## 7. Cautions & Disclaimers

> [!WARNING]
> **CIBRC & Grape Export NRCG MRL Registration Confirmation Required:** Chemical pesticide dosages, formulations, water volumes, and PHIs cited from government handbooks must be confirmed against current CIBRC approved product labels and ICAR-NRCG (National Research Centre for Grapes) Annexure-5 MRL export advisories before field application in Telangana (PJTSAU) and Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU).

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Missing-Dose Flag:** PPQS lists a dash (-) for the PHI of Captan 50% WP on grapes; the PHI is explicitly missing rather than unrestricted. Regional foliar correction doses for Copper and Manganese in grape soils were omitted in open official sources and are explicitly flagged as missing.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Export PHI Compliance:** Strict adherence to Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) is mandatory for both domestic and export grapes to prevent chemical residue interceptions at markets.
