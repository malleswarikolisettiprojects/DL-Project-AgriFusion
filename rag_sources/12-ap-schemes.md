# Andhra Pradesh Farmer Schemes — Verified RAG Source

## Scope
This source covers farmer-support schemes and department programmes relevant to farmers in **Andhra Pradesh** (with applicability to central schemes operating in neighbouring Telangana). It includes: **Annadatha Sukhibhava–PM KISAN**, **PM-KISAN**, **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**, **Andhra Pradesh Micro Irrigation Project (APMIP)**, AP subsidised seed distribution/Seed Village activity, Soil Health Card and micronutrient support, farm mechanisation, and Rythu Bharosa Kendralu (RBKs).

## Structured Scheme Entries

### 1. Annadatha Sukhibhava – PM KISAN (Andhra Pradesh)
- **Identity & Purpose:** AP flagship initiative supplementing landholding farmer families. Announced benefit: **₹20,000 per annum per farmer family**, which includes the **₹6,000** central PM-KISAN component. Landless agricultural cultivators are stated to receive **₹20,000/year** from the State budget.
- **Coverage:** Farmer families collectively owning cultivable land, ROFR land cultivators, and landless agricultural cultivators under CCRC cards.
- **Application & Status:** Verification via Aadhaar on [annadathasukhibhava.ap.gov.in](https://annadathasukhibhava.ap.gov.in/index.html). Crop Cultivator Rights Cards (CCRCs) are issued to tenant farmers for 11 months by mutual agreement of landowner and cultivator.

### 2. PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)
- **Benefit:** ₹6,000 per year per eligible landholding family in 3 equal instalments of ₹2,000.
- **Eligibility & Exclusions:** Landholding in farmer's own name as of 1 Feb 2019 cut-off. Excludes institutional owners, constitutional post holders, govt employees, income tax payers, and registered professionals (doctors, CAs, lawyers).
- **Mandatory Requirements:** Aadhaar authentication, bank account linked with Aadhaar, mandatory e-KYC.

### 3. Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
- **Coverage:** Farmers growing notified crops in notified areas.
- **Premium Ceilings:** 2% for Kharif food/oilseed crops, 1.5% for Rabi food/oilseed crops, 5% for commercial/horticultural crops.
- **Helpline & Portal:** Helpline 14447; Krishi Rakshak Portal & [pmfby.gov.in](https://pmfby.gov.in/).

### 4. AP Micro Irrigation Project (APMIP) / PMKSY-PDMC
- **Subsidy Pattern (AP GO No. 493):**
  - Small / Marginal Farmers (< 5 acres): 90% subsidy on Drip, 55% on Sprinkler.
  - Medium Farmers (5–10 acres): 70% subsidy on Drip, 45% on Sprinkler.
- **Application Route:** Apply via Rythu Seva Kendras (RSKs). Toll-free number: 1800 425 2960.

### 5. Rythu Bharosa Kendralu (RBKs) & Seed Subsidies
- **RBKs:** One-stop village-level centres for certified seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, digital kiosks, and agricultural advisories.
- **Seed Subsidies:** Distributed via APSSDCL under RKVY, NFSM, and Seed Village schemes.
