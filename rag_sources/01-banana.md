# Banana (Musa spp. / అరటి) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (Khammam, Nizamabad, Nalgonda districts - PJTSAU) & Andhra Pradesh (Guntur, West Godavari, East Godavari, Anantapur, YSR Kadapa districts - ANGRAU / Dr. YSRHU), India  
**Crop Species:** *Musa acuminata* / *Musa balbisiana* (Banana / Plantain / అరటి)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu):** అరటి (Arati / Banana), అరటి తోట (Arati Thota / Plantation), అరటి గెల (Arati Gela / Bunch), అరటి పిల్లలు (Arati Pillalu / Suckers)  
**Commercial Cultivars (AP/Telangana):** Grand Naine (G9 - Tissue Culture), Robusta, Dwarf Cavendish, Karpura Poovan, Amritapani (Rasthali), Red Banana.

---

## 1. Plantation Establishment & Planting Systems

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Deep, rich, well-drained clay loams, silty clay loams, and alluvial soils high in organic matter. Sensitive to waterlogging, poor drainage, and soil salinity. Optimal soil pH: 6.5 – 7.5. Electrical Conductivity (EC) must be < 1.0 dS/m.
- **Planting Seasons:**
  - **Kharif Planting:** June – July (with onset of South-West Monsoon).
  - **Post-Monsoon Planting:** October – November.

### Planting Material & Treatment
- **Planting Material:** Pathogen-free Tissue Culture (TC) plantlets or healthy Sword Suckers weighing 500 – 750 g.
- **Sucker Paring & Pralinage Protocol:**
  - Trim off old dead roots and decayed corm portions (Paring).
  - Dip corms in a slurry mixture of 50 g clay + 10 g Carbofuran 3% CG or 100 g Neem cake + *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/corm in 1 L water to prevent Burrowing Nematodes (*Radopholus similis*) and Rhizome Weevils.

### Spacing & Density
- **Grand Naine (G9 - TC):** 1.8 m x 1.5 m (Plant Density: ~3,700 plants/ha) or 1.5 m x 1.5 m (4,444 plants/ha under high density planting).
- **Tall Cultivars (Poovan / Rasthali):** 2.1 m x 2.1 m or 2.4 m x 2.4 m.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Annual NPK Fertilizer Rates per Plant
- **Organic Manure / FYM:** Apply 10 – 15 kg FYM / Compost + 1 kg Neem Cake per plant at planting.
- **Annual Chemical NPK Fertilizer Dosage per Plant (Grand Naine High-Yielding TC):**
  - **Total Annual Requirement:** **200 g Nitrogen (N) : 50 g Phosphorus (P2O5) : 300 g Potassium (K2O)** per plant per crop cycle.
- **Drip Fertigation Schedule (Split across Growth Stages):**
  - **Vegetative Phase (1st to 4th Month):** Apply 100 g N, 50 g P2O5, and 50 g K2O per plant in weekly splits.
  - **Flower Initiation / Shooting Phase (5th to 8th Month):** Apply 100 g N and 150 g K2O per plant in weekly splits.
  - **Bunch Development Phase (9th to 11th Month):** Apply remaining 100 g K2O per plant in weekly splits.

### Micronutrient Management & Deficiency Corrections
- **Foliar Micronutrient Cocktail Spray (Banana Special / TNAU Formula):**
  - **Spray Formulation:** **Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 0.5% (5 g/L) + Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 0.2% (2 g/L) + Boric Acid @ 0.1% (1 g/L) + Urea @ 1.0% (10 g/L)**. *(Note: Extension guides vary between 0.2% and 0.5% ZnSO4; confirm with local KVK based on leaf analysis)*.
  - **Timing:** Spray 3 times — at 3rd, 5th, and 7th months after planting.
- **Magnesium Deficiency (Interveinal Margins Yellowing):**
  - Soil application of Magnesium Sulphate (MgSO4) @ 25 kg/ha or foliar spray @ 0.5% (5 g/L). *(NHB notes foliar MgSO4 but specifies no concentration; dose confirmed via TNAU extension)*.

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Pests

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Banana Stem Weevil (*Odoiporus longicollis*) & Rhizome Weevil (*Cosmopolites sordidus*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Stem Weevil larvae bore into pseudostem producing pinholes exuding gummy fluid; pseudostem turns spongy and snaps during high winds. Rhizome Weevil larvae tunnel into underground corms causing leaf yellowing and stunting.
- **Mechanical Control:** Keep plantation weed-free. Lay split pseudostem traps (30 cm long split logs) @ 100 traps/ha; collect and destroy adult beetles daily.
- **Biological Option:** Swab split pseudostem traps with *Beauveria bassiana* @ 5 g/L or entomopathogenic nematodes (*Heterorhabditis bacteriophora*).
- **Chemical Option:** Pseudostem injection or swabbing with **Chlorpyrifos 20% EC @ 4 mL/L** (or 54 mL Chlorpyrifos + 350 mL water per 100 trees injected at 30 cm and 60 cm height) or Carbaryl 50% WP @ 3 g/L.

#### B. Banana Aphid (*Pentalonia nigronervosa*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck sap from leaf bases and pseudostem crown; vector for the lethal Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV).
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L** or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 2 mL/L targeted into leaf axils and pseudostem bases.

#### C. Burrowing Nematode (*Radopholus similis*) & Root-Knot Nematode (*Meloidogyne incognita*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Dark reddish-brown necrotic lesions on roots, root decay, unanchored plants tipping over during heavy fruit bearing ("Toppling Disease").
- **Biological Option:** Soil application of *Paecilomyces lilacinus* or *Trichoderma viride* @ 25 g/plant mixed in FYM at planting.
- **Chemical Option:** Apply Carbofuran 3% CG @ 40 g/plant around corm base.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Sigatoka Leaf Spot / Yellow & Black Sigatoka (*Mycosphaerella musicola* / *M. fijiensis*)
- **Symptoms:** Pale yellow or greyish spindle-shaped spots with dark reddish-brown margins coalescing to cause extensive leaf drying, premature leaf drop, and small unripened fruits.
- **Organic Option:** Prune and burn severely spot-infected lower leaves. Spray Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L) + Mineral Oil / White Oil @ 10 mL/L.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Propiconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L** (mixed with 5 mL Mineral Oil/L water) or Difenoconazole 25% EC @ 1 mL/L or Azoxystrobin 23% SC @ 1 mL/L or Mancozeb 75% WP @ 2.5 g/L. Repeat at 25–30 day intervals.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Propiconazole 25% EC: 30 days waiting period; Difenoconazole: 30 days.

### B. Panama Wilt / Fusarium Wilt (*Fusarium oxysporum* f. sp. *cubense* TR4 / Race 1)
- **Symptoms:** Yellowing of lower leaf margins progressing to upper leaves, petiole buckling with leaves hanging like a skirt around pseudostem; longitudinal splitting of pseudostem base; reddish-brown vascular discoloration in corm.
- **Management:** Plant resistant Grand Naine (G9) cultivar (resistant to Race 1). Soil drenching and corm injection with *Pseudomonas fluorescens* @ 10 g/L or Carbendazim 50% WP @ 2 g/L (2 mL of 2% Carbendazim solution corm injection). Eradicate TR4 infected plants using Urea and Bleaching powder.

### C. Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV)
- **Symptoms:** Narrow, upright, bunched rosette leaves at crown with dark green "dot-dash" streaks on leaf veins ("Morse code" pattern); complete stunting, failure to bear bunches.
- **Management:** Rogue out infected plants immediately, drench with kerosene/insecticide, and destroy. Control Aphid vector (*Pentalonia nigronervosa*) using Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.3 mL/L. No chemical cure exists.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - Harvest bunches when top angular ridges of fingers become smooth and rounded, flower ends dry off easily, and fruits reach **75–80% green maturity** for long-distance transport (90–100 days post-flowering).
- **Bunch Sleeving:** Cover bunches with 6% perforated blue polyethylene sleeve bags after complete hand formation to protect fruits from sunscald, thrips, and mechanical scratches.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Safety Wait:**
  - **Propiconazole 25% EC / Difenoconazole 25% EC:** 30-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL:** 21–30 days waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Neem, Mineral Oil, Trichoderma):** 0–1 day safety margin.
- **Post-Harvest Handling:** Cut bunches using secateurs leaving a 30 cm peduncle handle. De-hand bunches, wash in 0.1% Alum solution to remove latex, shade dry, and pack in ventilated corrugated fiberboard (CFB) boxes.

> **CIBRC & Label Registration Confirmation Required:** Chemical pesticide dosages, formulations, water volumes, and PHIs cited from government handbooks must be confirmed against current CIBRC approved product labels before field application in Telangana (PJTSAU) and Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU / Dr. YSRHU).

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Missing-Dose Flag:** NHB notes Magnesium deficiency foliar MgSO4 but specifies no concentration; no dose was assumed. TNAU pages differ on ZnSO4 spray concentration (0.5% vs 0.2%); confirm via leaf analysis and local KVK.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Virus Vector Control:** Chemical sprays do not cure Banana Bunchy Top Virus (BBTV). Rogue out infected plants immediately and control Aphid vectors strictly.
