# Orange / Citrus (Sweet Orange - Sathgudi / Bathayi / బత్తాయి) Crop Production & Protection Guide
**Target Region:** Telangana (Nalgonda, Nagarkurnool, Wanaparthy districts - PJTSAU) & Andhra Pradesh (Anantapur, YSR Kadapa, Nellore districts - ANGRAU / Dr. YSRHU Citrus Research Station Petlur / Tirupati), India  
**Crop Species:** *Citrus sinensis* L. Osbeck (Sweet Orange / Sathgudi / Mosambi) & *Citrus reticulata* Blanco (Mandarin Orange)  
**Local Terminology (Telugu/Hindi):** బత్తాయి (Bathayi / Sweet Orange), చీని (Cheeni / Sathgudi), కమలా (Kamala / Mandarin), సంత్రా (Santra)

---

## 1. Orchard Establishment & Bahar Management

### Soil & Climate Requirements
- **Soil Requirement:** Deep, well-drained sandy loams, red loamy soils, and alluvial soils with good organic matter. Highly sensitive to waterlogging and soil salinity/alkalinity. Optimal soil pH: 6.5 – 7.5. Electrical Conductivity (EC) must be < 1.0 dS/m.
- **Rootstock Recommendation:** **Rangpur Lime** (*Citrus limonia*) rootstock is strongly recommended in AP and Telangana for Sathgudi sweet orange to ensure tolerance to Citrus Tristeza Virus (CTV), drought, and root rot.
- **Spacing:** 6.0 m x 6.0 m (Plant Density: ~277 trees/ha) or 7.0 m x 7.0 m (204 trees/ha).

### Bahar Management (Flower Regulation in South India)
- **Mrig Bahar (June–July Flowering):** The primary commercial bahar for Sathgudi / Sweet Orange in AP and Telangana. Water is withheld during April–May for 45–60 days until leaf stress appears. Irrigation is resumed with onset of monsoon to induce mass flowering. Fruit harvest: February – March.
- **Ambe Bahar (January–February Flowering):** Water withheld in December–January. Fruit harvest: September – October.
- **Hasta Bahar (September–October Flowering):** Water withheld in August. Fruit harvest: March – April.

## 2. Nutrient Management & Fertilizer Dosages

### Annual NPK Fertilizer Rates per Bearing Tree (> 6 Years Old)
- **Farm Yard Manure (FYM):** Apply FYM / Organic Compost @ 50 – 75 kg/tree/year + 5 kg Neem Cake per tree in December/January.
- **Annual Chemical NPK Fertilizer Dosage per Tree:**
  - Total Annual Dose: **600 g Nitrogen (N) : 200 g Phosphorus (P2O5) : 400 g Potassium (K2O)** per tree per year.
- **Split Application Schedule:**
  - **1st Split (Post-Water Stress / Flowering - June):** Apply 50% N (300 g) + 100% P2O5 (200 g) + 50% K2O (200 g) per tree.
  - **2nd Split (Fruit Development Stage - September):** Apply remaining 50% N (300 g) + remaining 50% K2O (200 g) per tree.

### Micronutrient Management & Fruit Drop Prevention
- **Foliar Micronutrient Cocktail Spray:**
  - **Spray Formulation:** **Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4 21%) @ 0.5% (5 g/L) + Ferrous Sulphate (FeSO4) @ 0.5% (5 g/L) + Manganese Sulphate (MnSO4) @ 0.2% (2 g/L) + Boric Acid @ 0.1% (1 g/L) + Urea @ 1.0% (10 g/L)**.
  - **Application Timing:** Spray twice during new flush emergence (July and October).
- **Pre-Harvest Fruit Drop Prevention:**
  - Spray **2,4-D Sodium Salt 80% WP @ 10–15 ppm** (1.0 – 1.5 g formulation per 100 L water) or NAA @ 20 ppm + Zinc Sulphate 0.5% at marble fruit size stage (30–45 days post-fruit set) and repeat 30 days before harvest. *(CIBRC label confirmation required)*.
- *[Missing-Dose Flag: Specific regional foliar correction doses for Magnesium and Copper in alkaline Sathgudi soils of AP/Telangana are missing from reviewed extension advisories; consult local KVK before application.]*

## 3. Integrated Pest Management (IPM) & Insect Pests

### Major Insect Pests

#### A. Citrus Leaf Miner (*Phyllocnistis citrella*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Larvae mine between upper and lower epidermal layers of young leaves creating serpentine silvery winding trails; leaves curl, distort, and dry up. Leaf miner wounds serve as major entry points for Citrus Canker bacteria.
- **Organic Option:** Spray Neem Seed Kernel Extract (NSKE 5%) @ 50 g/L or Azadirachtin 1% EC @ 3 mL/L (10 mL/L for 0.03% EC formulation) on new flushes.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 mL/L** or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.25 g/L or Dimethoate 30% EC @ 2 mL/L during peak flush emergence.

#### B. Citrus Psylla (*Diaphorina citri*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs and adults suck cell sap from tender shoots causing leaf distortion and dieback; vector for the lethal Huanglongbing (HLB / Citrus Greening) disease caused by *Candidatus* Liberibacter.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.4 mL/L or Thiamethoxam 25% WG @ 0.25 g/L or Quinalphos 25% EC @ 2 mL/L.

#### C. Citrus Blackfly & Whitefly (*Aleurocanthus woglumi*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Nymphs crowd lower leaf surfaces; heavy honeydew secretion leads to severe black soot mold ("Kolambi") covering foliage and fruits, reducing photosynthesis.
- **Chemical Option:** Spray Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 mL/L or Acephate 75% SP @ 1.5 g/L or Neem Oil 3% (30 mL/L).

#### D. Fruit Sucking Moth (*Otheris fullonia* / *Eudocima maternal*)
- **Damage Symptoms:** Adult moths attack ripening fruits at night, drilling holes into rind and sucking juice, leading to secondary fungal decay and mass pre-harvest fruit drop.
- **Management:** Collect and destroy fallen fruits daily. Install Poison Bait Traps (Mix 50 g Jaggery + 10 mL Malathion 50% EC + 1 L water + fruit juice in wide-mouthed bottles hung in orchard). Hang Solar Light Traps @ 1/acre.

## 4. Disease Management Protocols

### A. Citrus Canker (*Xanthomonas citri* subsp. *citri*)
- **Symptoms:** Raised corky, rough brownish lesions with a yellow halo on leaves, twigs, green branches, and fruit rind; fruits become severely blemished and unmarketable.
- **Management:** Prune infected twigs before monsoon. Spray **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP @ 2.5 g/L** (1.25–2.5 kg formulation in 750–1,000 L water/ha) + **Streptocycline @ 0.1 g/L** at 30-day intervals during new flush / rainy season.
- *[Missing PHI Flag: PPQS fungicide table specifies no waiting period for Copper Oxychloride on citrus leaf spot/canker; PHI is explicitly missing and must be verified with local extension officer.]*

### B. Phytophthora Gummosis & Foot Rot (*Phytophthora nicotianae* / *P. citrophthora*)
- **Symptoms:** Bark cracking on lower trunk near ground level exuding dark brown gum resin ("Gummosis"); bark decays, roots rot leading to yellowing, dieback, and tree death.
- **Preventive Cultural Option:** Use Rangpur Lime rootstock with graft union > 30 cm above soil level. Paint trunk with Bordeaux Paste (1 kg CuSO4 + 1 kg Quick Lime + 10 L water).
- **Chemical Option:** Soil drenching around root zone with **Aureofungin 46.15% @ 1% formulation** (or Fosetyl-Al 80% WP @ 2.5 g/L or Metalaxyl 8% + Mancozeb 64% WP @ 2.5 g/L) in 750 L water/ha.
- **Pre-Harvest Interval (PHI):** Aureofungin 46.15%: 30 days waiting period.

### C. Citrus Tristeza Virus (CTV) & Huanglongbing (HLB / Citrus Greening)
- **Symptoms:** CTV causes stem pitting, vein clearing, decline; HLB causes blotchy mottling on leaves, yellow shoots, small lopsided sour fruits with aborted green seeds.
- **Management:** Plant certified disease-free budded plants grafted on Rangpur Lime. Control Aphid vectors (for CTV) and Psylla vectors (for HLB) using Imidacloprid 17.8% SL @ 0.5 mL/L. No chemical cure exists.

## 5. Harvest Cautions & Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI)

- **Harvest Stage Identification:**
  - Harvest Sathgudi / Sweet Orange when fruit skin color changes from dark green to light yellowish-green and Total Soluble Solids (TSS) reach **10–12° Brix**.
- **Pre-Harvest Intervals (PHI) & Safety Wait:**
  - **Aureofungin 46.15%:** 30-day PHI waiting period.
  - **Copper Oxychloride 50% WP:** PHI missing in PPQS table *(Observe strict safety wait before harvest)*.
  - **Imidacloprid 17.8% SL / Thiamethoxam 25% WG:** 21–30 days waiting period.
  - **Bio-pesticides (Neem, Trichoderma, Pseudomonas):** 0–1 day safety margin.
- **Harvesting Technique:** Harvest fruits using sharp secateurs leaving a short pedicel attached. Do not pull fruits to avoid stem-end skin tearing and post-harvest rot. Store in cool ventilated sheds at 5–8°C and 85–90% Relative Humidity.

## 7. Cautions & Disclaimers

> [!WARNING]
> **CIBRC & Label Registration Confirmation Required:** Chemical pesticide dosages, formulations, water volumes, and PHIs cited from government handbooks must be confirmed against current CIBRC approved product labels before field application in Telangana (PJTSAU) and Andhra Pradesh (ANGRAU / Dr. YSRHU).

> [!NOTE]
> **Explicit Missing-Dose Flag:** PPQS lists no waiting period for Copper Oxychloride on citrus leaf spot/canker; the PHI is explicitly missing and must be verified with an extension officer. Regional foliar correction doses for Magnesium and Copper in citrus soils were omitted in reviewed open official sources and are explicitly flagged as missing.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Virus Vector Control:** Chemical sprays do not cure Citrus Tristeza Virus or Greening (HLB). Rogue out declining trees and control vector insects (Aphids & Psylla) strictly.
